The Smith suite

Small, fast video tools. These demos run entirely in your browser with ffmpeg.wasm: pick a file, nothing is uploaded, no account, no server.

ClipSmithTrim, reframe and export MP4, MP3 or GIF. GifSmithTurn any moment into a loop or boomerang GIF. CaptionSmithStyle captions and burn them into the video.

The first export downloads the video engine once (~31 MB, split into two files) and the browser caches it. Desktop apps add native-speed encoding, local AI transcription and full-length exports.
